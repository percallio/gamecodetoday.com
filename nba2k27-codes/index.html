How to Redeem

1. In MyTeam: go to the Market tab and scroll down to the Locker Codes section at the bottom of the screen. 2. In MyCareer (The City / MyCourt): open the menu, cycle to the Options/Quit tab on the right — Locker Codes sit at the bottom of that submenu. 3. Type the code with its dashes exactly as shown and confirm — case doesn't matter (upper or lower works); one redemption per account. Packs land in MyTeam's Unopened Packs tab automatically. You can also redeem from the official MyNBA2K companion app if you're away from the console/PC.
