How to Redeem

1. Launch Life in Olympus on Roblox. 2. Click the Rewards button in the top-right corner of the screen, then the CODES button. 3. Paste the code exactly as shown (codes are case-sensitive) into the "Enter a code" box and click REDEEM. 4. Check your Drachma / Reroll Token balance to confirm the reward arrived. Each code works once per account.
